Domestique your AI ride-planning teammate

Tell it where you want to ride and when. It plans the route, reads the wind hour by hour, finds water and coffee on the way, sets your tire pressure, and tells you how long you'll be out.

Try one

Tools it can call

  • plan_route BRouter bike routing, climbing, surface mix
  • get_ride_forecast hourly wind, rain, temps, daylight
  • analyze_headwind wind vs. your direction, mile by mile original
  • find_refuel_stops water, shops, cafes along the route
  • recommend_tire_pressure Berto 15%-drop model original
  • estimate_ride_effort physics: time, kJ, fueling
Every tool call the agent makes is shown inline. Click one to open the result. New session
How to use it: ask like you'd text a riding buddy. Name a start and destination and it will route it. Mention a day or time and it will pull that hour's wind. Give your weight, tires or watts and it will use them; otherwise it assumes a 165 lb rider at 150 W and says so. It remembers the conversation, so follow-ups like "where can I refill on that route?" work.
